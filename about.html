About ALHEZ

Your Trusted Partner in Medical, Scientific & Laboratory Supplies

ALHEZ is a Tanzania-based medical, laboratory, and scientific supplies company focused on delivering reliable, quality, and cost-effective solutions to healthcare facilities and education sectors. We supply a comprehensive range of medical equipment, hospital consumables, surgical instruments, diagnostic devices, reagents, and veterinary products.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, quality, and cost-effective medical, laboratory, scientific, veterinary, and educational products that support better healthcare delivery, professional practice, research, and learning.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and leading provider of integrated medical, laboratory, scientific, veterinary, and educational supply solutions in Tanzania and beyond.

  • Quality & Reliability: Sourced from trusted global manufacturers
  • Comprehensive Solutions: Diverse healthcare & education catalogue
  • Customer Focus: Practical, fit-for-purpose solutions
  • Integrity & Transparency: Long-term institutional partnerships
