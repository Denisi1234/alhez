What products does ALHEZ Medical Supplies supply?

ALHEZ supplies medical and hospital equipment, laboratory equipment and consumables, surgical instruments, diagnostic devices, dental supplies, PPE, medical disposables, veterinary supplies, and educational and scientific products.

Where is ALHEZ Medical Supplies Limited located?

ALHEZ Medical Supplies Limited is based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania (P.O. Box 14244). We serve institutions nationwide.

Does ALHEZ deliver outside Dar es Salaam?

Yes. ALHEZ supports healthcare providers, laboratories, universities, colleges, schools, NGOs, and public institutions across Tanzania, not only in Dar es Salaam.
